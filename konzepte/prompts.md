# FIRSTWERK – Storyboard-Prompts (sjinn Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9, 2K, Unlimited)

Gemeinsamer Stil (in jedem Prompt): 3x3-Raster, 9 fotorealistische Screen-Zustände derselben Webseite, fiktiver Dachdeckerbetrieb FIRSTWERK Bedachungen.
Handwerks-Regeln: Leitmotiv eindeutig Dachdecker, Film ↔ Motion Design, Vorher → Arbeit → Ergebnis, Recruiting-Kapitel (Film + 60-s-Bewerbung), Vorteile als Kinetik-Typo, Bewertungen nur „Beispiel“.

---

## FWSB-1 „Eindecken“ (Zeitraffer: altes Dach → neues Dach)

FWSB-1 Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end screen state of the same award-winning website for a fictional German roofing company called FIRSTWERK (Bedachungen). Cinematic lighting, real photography inside the web design, premium Awwwards-style layout, bold modern grotesk sans-serif typography in white, dark anthracite color world with brick red clay and copper as accent colors. Panels 1 to 5 show the exact same detached German house from the exact same fixed camera angle. Panel 1: full-screen hero at dusk, the house has an old weathered roof with moss, broken and missing clay tiles, small top navigation with the word FIRSTWERK, white headline "Dein Dach. Scroll mal." and a thin progress bar at 0 percent. Panel 2: same house, a scaffold with red safety nets and fall protection railing around the roof, a small truck-mounted crane lifts a pallet of new tiles, two roofers in work clothes and helmets remove the old tiles, step label "1 Abdecken", progress 25 percent. Panel 3: same house, bare wooden rafters, a white roofing membrane and fresh light wooden battens being nailed in neat parallel rows, step label "2 Dämmung und Lattung", progress 50 percent. Panel 4: same house, roofers lay new dark anthracite clay tiles row by row from the eaves upward, the lower half of the roof finished, step label "3 Eindecken", progress 75 percent. Panel 5: same house, finished new anthracite roof with shiny copper gutters and a roof window, warm golden sunset, white headline "Dicht. Für Generationen.", progress 100 percent. Panel 6: motion design services section on dark background, five photorealistic 3D clay roof tiles fanning out like cards, each tile engraved with one word: "Steildach", "Flachdach", "Dachfenster", "Solar", "Klempnerei". Panel 7: section "Dach-Check per Foto": a hand holds a smartphone photographing a roof, glowing orange outline and a square-meter label drawn on the roof in the camera view, three steps "Foto", "Adresse", "Rückruf". Panel 8: careers film chapter, a confident young roofer in his early twenties in a safety harness kneels on a roof ridge at sunrise above the city, huge kinetic white words sliding in one by one: "Führerschein.", "Firmenwagen.", "Meister? Zahlen wir." Panel 9: left a hand holding a smartphone with a swipe-card job application and a big button "In 60 Sekunden bewerben", right a review section with a large number "4,9", five gold stars filling up and small label "Beispiel". Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels, no real brand logos, no prices, no euro signs.
